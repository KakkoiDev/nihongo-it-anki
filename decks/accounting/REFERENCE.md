# 会計・お金周り / Japanese Accounting - deck source

129 terms across 9 tiers, covering the vocabulary needed to read and discuss the money/accounting flow of the [Financial feature enhancements](https://app.notion.com/p/meetsmore/Financial-feature-enhancements-31e1ff9969228048b256d2fef6c69a05) project with Japanese colleagues.

One short polite sentence per term (the term is the cloze). Card types: Listening (audio), Reading (JP text), Vocabulary (cloze with English hint). Readings verified against UniDic via `scripts/check_pronunciation.py`.
