## Judgment calls: maintainer decision, not auto-fixed

1. 13 no-blank clozes (all v5.0): Cloze is a lemma/phrase form absent
   from the Sentence, so Card 3's JS blanking (split/join on the literal
   cloze string) silently no-ops; the "blank" card shows the full
   sentence. Rows: t3:204 (速い/速く), t4:166 (フィルター/フィルタリング),
   t4:198 (重複排除/重複を排除), t6:39,45,78,81,82,83,84,87,95 (concept
   lemmas like 協力する over 協力してください), t9:20 (腐る/腐りやすい).
   Options: (a) re-cloze to the surface form appearing in the sentence
   (requires matching PitchAccent base updates; GUID-safe), or (b) accept
   as a deliberate "concept cloze" class like the documented
   abbreviation-expansion decisions. The t6 cluster reads like deliberate
   vocab-list lemmas; the t3/t4 conjugation cases look accidental.
   Recommendation: fix t3:204, t4:166, t4:198; declare t6/t9 a kept
   decision in IMPROVEMENTS.md either way.
2. Tier 4 tekudasai density is 36%, above the ~30% ceiling from
   IMPROVEMENTS item 9. Pre-existing, released. Leave or diversify ~12
   sentences in a future content pass (sentence edits on released tiers
   need the guid-migration-map flow).
3. No-pitch-coloring rows (plain rt, no pitch spans): 845/1516 deck-wide
   including 83/140 in t13-15. Consistent with the existing convention
   for UniDic-unresolvable compounds, so not treated as a defect. If
   pitch coloring for compounds is ever wanted, it's a generator feature,
   not a data fix.
